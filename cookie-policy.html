LEGAL / COOKIES

Cookies, without the mystery.

Last updated: September 2026

VENGLIC uses limited browser storage to make the website work and remember your consent choices.

Essential storage

Essential storage supports core website behavior, including remembering your cookie-consent choice. It is always enabled because disabling it can affect the site's basic operation.

Functional preferences

Optional functional storage can be enabled through the preference modal. It is intended for non-essential interface preferences when such features are implemented.

Analytics

Analytics is an optional category. The current VENGLIC page does not load an analytics service by default. If an analytics provider is added later, this policy should be updated with the provider, purpose, retention and opt-out information.

Managing your choices

Use “Cookie preferences” in the website footer to review or change your choices. You can also clear site storage through your browser settings.
