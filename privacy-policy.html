When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, institution, role, enquiry type, message and any date, time or address details you choose to provide.

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and applicable business or legal requirements. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards should be used to protect it.

Your choices

You can choose whether to allow optional cookie categories through the site's preference controls. You can also contact VENGLIC to ask about information associated with your enquiry, subject to applicable legal requirements.
